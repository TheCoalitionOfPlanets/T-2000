Make yourself heard.

  1. Bring your voice. Record or upload 10–20 seconds of clear, single-speaker speech, up to 30 seconds. Listen back for noise, clipping, and long silences. Shorter clips still work.
  2. Clean up and trim. Preview background noise reduction and start/end edits, then compare with the original. Editing a saved voice makes a new copy.
  3. Check the words. Saving transcribes your reference automatically. Review the transcript and correct any words the recognizer missed.
  4. Make it speak. Prepare your saved voice, enter new words, and generate. Each completed phrase plays as it arrives.

Your recording guides the voice color. Use Speaking speed for pace, and listen for pitch and delivery differences.
